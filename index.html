How it works

Seven days of acute triage, then 28 days of rebuilding

Work through each stage at your own pace. There’s no need to rush ahead.

  1. Part 1 · Acute Triage (Days 1–7)

    Calm the physical ache, stop reaching for your phone, and break the 2 a.m. mental loop.

  2. Part 2.1 · Reflect and Reset (Days 8–14)

    Clear emotional landmines, strip away the highlight reel, and see the relationship for what it really was.

  3. Part 2.2 · Rebuild Confidence (Days 15–21)

    Small, daily manageable wins to reconnect with your worth and regain control of your attention.

  4. Part 2.3 · Everyday Foundations (Days 22–28)

    Restore sleep, appetite, and body rhythms to rebuild the practical ground beneath your feet.

  5. Part 2.4 · Looking Ahead (Days 29–35)

    Raise your standards, set unbreakable boundaries, and walk forward on your own terms.

Questions

What people ask first

Will this make the breakup hurt less?

No. Grief follows its own timeline, and this protocol can’t make it disappear. It offers a little structure and practical support while you move through it.

I still want my ex back. Should I still do this?

Yes, and do it first. Whatever you decide later—whether to walk away for good or navigate future conversations—you need to decide from a grounded, clear, and self-respecting place, not from panic and withdrawal. When you aren’t running on craving, you take back your own power.

What if I miss a few days?

Nothing expires, and you can pick up where you left off. There are no streaks to maintain or days to make up.

How long is a lesson?

Most audio lessons are about 15 minutes, with a short activity that usually takes 5 to 20 minutes. You can also read the lesson instead.

Do I have to talk to anyone?

No. No calls, no live sessions, no forums. Nobody watches you use it.
